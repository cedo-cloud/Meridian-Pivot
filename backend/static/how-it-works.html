Why a badge can never print twice

Printers are slow, sometimes offline, and never instant. A check-in system that assumes the printer will always answer right away breaks the moment it doesn't — either the screen lies about what's actually printed, or a second scan sneaks a duplicate badge through while the first one is still working.

Solstice Check-In is built around a queue-and-confirm model instead. A scan doesn't print anything directly — it queues a print job and waits for the printer to actually confirm it's done, however long that takes. The screen only ever shows what's true right now: pending while a badge is printing, checked in only once it's confirmed.

Duplicate protection that holds under real conditions

Two problems come up once printing is no longer instant: a desk can get scanned twice before the first badge finishes, and confirmations for different attendees can come back in a different order than they were scanned in — busier printers process jobs on their own schedule, not first-in-first-out.

Every scan gets its own job. An attendee only has one active job at a time, and a printer confirmation only counts if it matches that attendee's current job — a stale or repeated confirmation is safely ignored rather than double-counted. That's what makes it safe for a second scan of an already-printing or already-checked-in badge to be rejected instantly, and for print confirmations to complete in any order without ever mixing up who got checked in.

What happens when a print actually fails

If the printer reports a failure instead of a success, the attendee isn't left stuck on pending forever — the badge is released back to "not checked in" so staff can simply rescan it.
