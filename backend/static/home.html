Badge check-in that never double-prints.

Solstice Check-In runs registration for multi-day conferences: scan a badge, print it, done. It's built around a simple rule — the screen never says "Checked In" until the badge has actually printed, and no attendee can ever get printed twice, no matter how many times a desk gets scanned or how the printer responds.
